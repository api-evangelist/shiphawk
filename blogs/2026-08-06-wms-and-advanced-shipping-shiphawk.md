A WMS and advanced shipping software solve different problems. A WMS manages what happens inside your warehouse: receiving, putaway, directed picking, packing, and inventory accuracy. Advanced shipping software manages what happens at the point of shipment: carrier selection, rate optimization, label generation, and freight and parcel audit.
