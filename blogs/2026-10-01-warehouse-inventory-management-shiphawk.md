A pre-peak audit for operations teams that need accurate, fast-moving warehouse inventory before Q4 volume arrives Peak-ready warehouse inventory heading into Q4 2026 means your system matches your shelves, your fastest movers can absorb demand spikes, and a seasonal hire on their first shift can find and pick the right item without tribal knowledge. Getting there starts with an audit of receiving, bin-level tracking, cycle counting, replenishment, and the control methods behind your reorder decisions.
